Asteroid Destroyer Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 20, 2026

Asteroid Destroyer does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

The game may store gameplay information, such as high scores and settings, locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Asteroid Destroyer currently does not use:
- Advertising services
- Analytics services
- User accounts
- Third-party tracking
- Cloud storage of personal data

If the game's data practices change in a future update, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
